Où voir du Street Art à Rouen ?

Le Street-Art, appelé également art urbain, regroupe toutes les pratiques artistiques réalisées dans la rue ou dans des endroits publics. Il est le plus souvent éphémère, libre et accessible gratuitement à un large public. Rouen accueille de nombreux artistes qui habillent les rues et ruelles de la métropole normande.

Pam The Bird Trial

Jack Gibson-Burrell, accused of the Pam the Bird graffiti, is considering a holistic plea deal to resolve over 200 charges as he appeared in Melbourne Magistrates' Court. Jack Gibson-Burrell, the man accused of spray-painting Pam the Bird graffiti across Melbourne, is considering a plea deal to resolve all charges against him. His defence lawyer Micky Milardovic told Melbourne Magistrates' Court on Tuesday that a holistic offer would be put to prosecutors between now and November.
